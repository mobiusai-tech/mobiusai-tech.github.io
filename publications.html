Predict + Optimize: A New Paradigm in Fleet AI

The core of Mobius AI is built on over a decade of academic research in machine learning, optimization, and systems engineering. Unlike traditional Operations Research (OR) methods that solve for the current state, our approach is non-myopic—planning for thousands of possible futures and dynamically adjusting in real time.

This combination of predictive modeling and sequential decision-making is essential for handling the uncertainty and complexity of real-world fleet operations, especially in mixed and autonomous vehicle settings.
